# Content production workflow

1. **Idea:** Capture audience, claim, and evidence gap in the idea bank.
2. **Drafting:** Copy the brief to `drafts/YYYY-MM-DD-topic-platform.md`.
3. **Review:** Check accuracy, permissions, clarity, and accessibility.
4. **Ready:** Final copy and assets are approved.
5. **Scheduled:** Record channel and date in the brief.
6. **Published:** Add live URL and actual date.
7. **Learned:** Review results at 7 and 30 days; record the next experiment.

## Weekly rhythm
Choose 2–3 ideas with a specific reader problem and real evidence. Build one anchor piece, then adapt it to formats that fit. Review meaningful comments and conversations alongside reach.

## Measurement
Record platform, format, publish date, URL, reach, saves/shares, substantive replies, and qualified conversations. Compare similar formats on the same platform. Do not infer causation from a post preceding a business result.
