# Editorial guide

Write with a clear point of view, concrete examples, and plain language. Each piece should serve one audience and make one argument. Lead with a specific observation, then show the evidence and practical implication.

## Quality bar
- State what the reader can do or understand differently.
- Verify claims, metrics, dates, quotes, and links before publishing.
- Use only examples and company information approved for public use.
- Keep paragraphs short. Remove jargon, generic inspiration, and unsupported certainty.
- Check that the final copy sounds natural when read aloud.

## AI assistance
Use AI to organize notes, generate options for a hook, critique clarity, and adapt an approved argument to another format. Require it to flag missing evidence, never fabricate it. Final editorial review belongs to the author.
