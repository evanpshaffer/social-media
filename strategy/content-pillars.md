# Content pillars

| Pillar | Audience | Example angles | Useful evidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GTM systems and AI | Revenue leaders | Point-of-need guidance, agent architecture, knowledge quality | Diagram, workflow, field example |
| Enablement and behavior change | Sales and enablement leaders | Manager reinforcement, champions, launch adoption | Approved program example and metrics |
| Building in public | Practitioners and collaborators | Decisions, experiments, lessons, tradeoffs | Artifact, prototype, before-and-after |
| Sustainable work practices | Professionals | Prioritization, routines, learning loops | Personal process and observed outcomes |

A pillar guides idea selection; it does not require a fixed posting quota. Score ideas 0–2 each for audience relevance, strength of evidence, original viewpoint, and timeliness.
